# Elfangary / الفنجري — Premium Smooth Web Design Spec

## Design Direction
Create a premium honey brand landing page inspired by the supplied hero reference, but adapted to the Elfangary / الفنجري identity and logo.
The overall feeling should be luxurious, smooth, warm, modern, and natural.
Avoid a generic grocery-store look; the experience should feel like a refined premium product brand.

## Brand Identity
- **Brand name (Arabic):** الفنجري
- **Brand name (Latin):** Elfangary
- **Category:** Natural honey / premium honey products
- **Tone:** premium, elegant, calm, trustworthy, organic
- **Core keywords:** smooth curves, warm glow, soft depth, clean luxury, golden simplicity

## Source Notes
- The logo image is the main brand anchor and should define the visual identity.
- The logo uses a strong orange background with soft cream lettering, which should become the base for the palette.
- The first image suggests a premium hero composition: large rounded frame, blurred natural background, bold left-aligned headline, product card floating on the right, and generous whitespace.

## Visual Style
Use large rounded corners, soft shadows, subtle glass depth, and smooth spacing throughout the interface.
The design should balance premium minimalism with rich natural warmth.
Motion, if added in implementation, should feel slow and buttery rather than playful or sharp.

## Color Palette
Primary palette derived from the provided logo and reference mood:

- **Brand Orange:** #F08010
- **Deep Honey Gold:** #F0F0F0
- **Warm Amber:** #303020
- **Cream:** #F08000
- **Soft Olive/Bee Nature Accent:** #B07000
- **Dark Luxury Text:** #1E1720
- **Muted Supporting Text:** #6B6470
- **Border/Soft Glass Line:** rgba(255,255,255,0.28)

### Suggested Usage
- Use **Brand Orange** as the main identity background and CTA highlight.
- Use **Cream** for logo-safe surfaces, headings on dark warm backgrounds, and premium contrast.
- Use **Deep Honey Gold** and **Warm Amber** in gradients, glow effects, and image overlays.
- Use dark text only on light cards and cream surfaces.
- Keep the interface saturated but controlled; avoid too many competing colors.

## Typography
Use a modern geometric sans-serif with smooth curves for English and a refined Arabic typeface with strong readability.
Recommended pairings:

- **Arabic:** Alexandria, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, or Cairo (prefer Alexandria for a more premium feel)
- **English:** Plus Jakarta Sans, Manrope, or Sora

### Type Rules
- Headlines: bold, large, clean, high contrast
- Body: medium weight, relaxed line-height
- Arabic headings should feel confident and elegant, not overly decorative
- Use slightly increased letter spacing only for small English labels, badges, or metadata

## Layout Structure

### 1. Header
A transparent or cream-tinted floating header inside the main rounded frame.

- Left: Elfangary logo
- Center: navigation links
- Right: account, cart, and primary CTA
- Container should feel inset within the hero shell, not edge-to-edge

Suggested nav items:
- Home
- Shop
- Our Honey
- Benefits
- Reviews
- FAQ
- Contact

### 2. Hero Section
This is the main emotional selling area.

**Layout:**
- Left side: headline, short subtext, CTA buttons
- Right side: featured floating product/info card
- Background: macro honey/bee/flower visual with strong blur depth and warm vignette
- Outer shell: oversized rounded rectangle with premium frame effect

**Headline direction examples:**
- Pure Honey, Crafted with Premium Care.
- Taste the Golden Standard of Natural Honey.
- الفنجري — عسل طبيعي بنقاء يليق بالذوق الرفيع

**Supporting text style:**
Keep the copy short, sensory, and trustworthy. Focus on purity, natural sourcing, richness, and premium taste.

**Primary CTA:** Shop Now
**Secondary CTA:** Discover Our Honey

### 3. Floating Product Card
A premium card overlapping the hero image on the right.

Include:
- 5-star rating row
- Product title
- Short offer or product descriptor
- 2–3 thumbnail previews
- Price
- Add to Cart button

Card style:
- Cream or soft white surface
- Rounded 24–28px corners
- Shadow with low blur and soft spread
- Very light border for glass-polish feel

### 4. Trust Strip
Directly below hero or integrated at the bottom of the hero frame.

Possible items:
- 100% Natural
- No Additives
- Premium Quality
- Fast Delivery
- Lab Tested / Trusted Source

Use pill badges or compact icon-text blocks.

### 5. Product Highlights
A premium grid for bestsellers or honey variants.

Each card should include:
- Product image
- Arabic + English title option
- Short tasting or benefit note
- Price
- CTA

Use consistent spacing and rounded card geometry.

### 6. Brand Story Section
Show craftsmanship, source, and trust.

Possible layout:
- Left: rich image block with rounded corners
- Right: brand story copy about purity, bee care, and quality selection

This section should feel editorial and elegant, with more breathing room than e-commerce-heavy blocks.

### 7. Benefits Section
Use 3 or 4 premium icon cards.

Example benefit topics:
- Naturally rich flavor
- Carefully sourced
- Daily wellness support
- Premium packaging

Avoid medical overclaim language.

### 8. Testimonials
Use soft cream cards with minimal text and strong spacing.
A premium honey brand should feel credible through calm, polished reviews rather than crowded social proof.

### 9. Footer
Use a darker honey-toned or deep olive surface for contrast.

Include:
- Logo
- Short brand statement
- Navigation links
- Social links
- Contact info
- Payment/shipping reassurance

## UI Styling Rules
- Border radius: 18px for buttons, 24px for cards, 32px+ for hero shell
- Buttons: pill-shaped or soft-rounded
- Shadows: subtle, wide blur, low opacity
- Avoid harsh black shadows
- Use gradients softly: orange to amber, cream to warm ivory
- Keep icon style minimal and thin
- Use whitespace generously

## Interaction Style
- Hover states should slightly lift cards and brighten highlights
- Buttons should animate with soft easing, around 200–300ms
- Avoid aggressive scaling or flashy motion
- Page transitions, if used, should fade/slide softly

## Imagery Direction
Use close-up visuals of bees, flowers, honey texture, honey jars, and golden liquid details.
All imagery should feel premium, warm, and cinematic rather than stock-heavy or cluttered.
Prefer shallow depth of field, warm highlights, and soft natural blur.

## Logo Usage
- Keep the provided logo as the main official identity reference
- Do not distort or over-outline it
- Prefer placing the logo on cream, white, or controlled orange backgrounds
- Maintain clear space around the mark
- If using a monochrome version, use cream on orange or dark honey text on cream

## Arabic/English Direction
The brand can support bilingual presentation.
Recommended approach:

- Primary public brand display: الفنجري
- Secondary support label: Elfangary
- Main headline can be Arabic with English secondary copy, or the reverse depending on audience
- Keep alignment intentional; avoid mixing Arabic and English randomly inside the same line

## Component Notes

### Buttons
- Primary: orange or dark luxury surface with cream text
- Secondary: ghost/cream style with subtle border
- Large CTA buttons should feel soft and premium, not sharp or corporate

### Input Fields
- Rounded, tall fields
- Light cream or white fill
- Thin warm-gray border
- Strong focus ring in soft orange glow

### Badges
- Use for “100% Natural”, “Best Seller”, “New Harvest”, “Premium”
- Keep them small, smooth, and refined

## Recommended Page Background
Use one of these patterns:

1. Soft warm gradient from honey gold to muted olive
2. Cream background with orange/gold spotlight sections
3. Blurred floral photography behind a framed hero container

Best choice for this direction: blurred nature photography behind a polished cream-framed hero area.
